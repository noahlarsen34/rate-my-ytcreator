Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Rate My Creator and explains how the application accesses, collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, retains, and deletes personal information and Google user data.

Effective November 5, 2026

Information collected by Rate My Creator

When you create an account, we collect your email address, first and last name, username, and birthdate. You may optionally provide a phone number and gender. We also collect content and activity you submit to Rate My Creator, including creator ratings, reviews, disclosures, follows, and votes on reviews.

Our service providers may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the application, including authentication records, timestamps, and browser or device information.

Google user data we access

If you use Google to sign in, Google provides your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile image. Rate My Creator never receives your Google password.

If you separately choose Connect YouTube, Rate My Creator requests read-only access to your YouTube account. This permits the application to retrieve the list of YouTube channels to which you subscribe, including their channel identifiers, names, and available thumbnails. Connecting YouTube is optional and is not required to browse public ratings or maintain a Rate My Creator account.

How we use Google user data

We use basic Google profile data to authenticate you, create or identify your Rate My Creator account, display your account information, and secure your session. We use YouTube subscription data only to match subscribed channel identifiers with creators in our database, create corresponding follows in your Rate My Creator account, display available community ratings for those creators, and synchronize this feature when you request it. We do not upload, edit, delete, subscribe to, or unsubscribe from content on your YouTube account.

How other information is used

We use non-Google account information to operate accounts, display community reviews and ratings, personalize account pages, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, troubleshoot failures, and improve user-facing Rate My Creator functionality.

Sharing and disclosure

Your username, ratings, reviews, votes, and creator follows may be visible to other Rate My Creator users as part of the community features. We use Supabase for authentication and database services, Vercel for application hosting, and Google for Google sign-in and the optional YouTube connection. These processors receive information only as necessary to provide infrastructure and functionality on our behalf.

We do not sell Google user data or other personal information. We do not transfer it to data brokers or information resellers, and we do not use or disclose it for targeted advertising, personalized advertising, retargeting, lending, or creditworthiness decisions.

AI and prohibited uses

Rate My Creator does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Rate My Creator does not use Google APIs or Google user data to create, facilitate, promote, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery or other sexually exploitative content.

Storage and protection

Account and application data is stored in Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL. The application uses HTTPS in transit, authenticated sessions, least-privilege access, database row-level security, and restricted server-side access. Google OAuth refresh credentials, when stored for the optional YouTube connection, are encrypted and are not exposed through the public application database. Although we maintain reasonable safeguards, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We retain your Rate My Creator account information while your account remains active and for only as long afterward as reasonably necessary to provide the service, address security or legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Reviews or other public contributions may be retained unless you request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Google authentication information is retained while your account remains connected. YouTube connection credentials and subscription-derived data are retained only while needed to provide the connected feature. Disconnecting YouTube stops future access and removes stored connection credentials. When information is no longer required for these purposes, we delete or anonymize it.

Your choices and controls

You may update available profile information through account settings. You may disconnect Rate My Creator through Google’s third-party connections page and through Rate My Creator’s disconnect control when available.

To request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal information and connected YouTube data, email noahlarsen018@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request belongs to the affected account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Rate My Creator’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features described in this policy.

Children’s privacy

Rate My Creator is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Rate My Creator or its use of Google user data changes. We will post the revised policy here, update the effective date, and provide additional notice when required.

Contact

Rate My Creator is operated as a class project. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to noahlarsen018@gmail.com.
